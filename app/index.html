NeuroTrace

Measure how you move.

NeuroTrace turns an ordinary phone into a motor-function measurement tool.

Machine learning models trained on clinical movement-disorder datasets extract motor features that clinicians look for in Parkinson's disease.

These are measurements of how you moved during a few short tasks, compared with other people who took the same tasks. They are not a diagnosis and cannot tell you whether you have a neurological condition.

Before you start

All analysis runs on the device. No motor data ever leaves the phone unless you separately choose to contribute it, which is off by default.
